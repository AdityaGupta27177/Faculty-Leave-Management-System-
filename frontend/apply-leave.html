Filing Guidelines
  • Advance Notice: Applications should ideally be submitted prior to the date of intended leave.
  • Casual Leaves (CL): Quota of 12 days per academic session. May not be combined with vacation without Dean approval.
  • Academic Duty (ADL): Conferred for university exams, conference presentations, and official committee duties.
  • Medical Sanctions: Supporting physician certificate is mandatory for sick leaves exceeding 2 consecutive days.
Institutional Sanctioning Flow

Your leave filing is dispatched to your Department Head (HOD) queue in real-time. Once sanctioned, your entitlement balance will be updated automatically.
